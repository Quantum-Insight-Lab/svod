Russian drone hits residential building in Romania

A Russian drone struck a residential building in the Romanian city of Galati overnight, the country's defence ministry said on Friday.

NATO condemned the strike as "reckless" and vowed to defend every inch of alliance territory.

"We will respond to any violation of allied airspace," a NATO spokesperson said.

Two people were wounded and a fire broke out, local emergency services reported.
